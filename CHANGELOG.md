# Changelog

All notable changes to zRAM Monitor are documented here.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-31

- Initial public release under the permanent `quest.entropy.zrammonitor` plugin ID.
- Connected visual map of logical zram contents, physical RAM cost, avoided RAM, and total system-memory use.
- Fifteen-minute zram traffic and PSI memory-pressure history.
- Plasma theme-aware compact icon and full display.
- Configurable refresh interval, pressure threshold, sustained-pressure duration, panel ratio, and notifications.
- Warning and critical panel-icon states.
